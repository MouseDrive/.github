# Getting help

1. **Open the Setup window** in MouseDrive. It checks every requirement (DLL, driver, device, axes, buttons) and says how to fix each failure.
2. **Read the README**: [Quick start](https://github.com/MouseDrive/MouseDrive#quick-start), [In-game settings](https://github.com/MouseDrive/MouseDrive#in-game-settings) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/MouseDrive/MouseDrive#troubleshooting).
3. **Ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/MouseDrive/MouseDrive/discussions/categories/q-a).** Mention your MouseDrive version and game, and paste the output of **Copy diagnostics** (bottom right of the main window).
4. **Found a bug?** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/MouseDrive/MouseDrive/issues/new/choose).

English or Turkish is fine.
